# NCTB AI Study Copilot

An early proof of concept for an AI study partner for students in grades 6–12 in Bangladesh. The tutor is designed to explain concepts at the student's level, cite the relevant NCTB textbook pages, and help students reason through homework with hints. It asks learners to explain ideas in their own words and uses their responses to decide what to revisit.

The first demo will focus on one Class 9–10 Physics chapter, starting with grounded explanations and adding a short mock exam and a sample progress report as the project develops.

The [development plan](DevelopmentPlan.md) contains the build phases, learning resources, and completion checks for anyone following or forking the project.
